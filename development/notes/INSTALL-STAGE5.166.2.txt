RUNTIME CONTROLS

- Touch anywhere during ordinary gameplay: OPEN journal only.
- Touch inside journal: no action; no on-screen TRACK/SCROLL/CLOSE buttons.
- Joystick UP/DOWN: highlight previous/next quest (scrolls as needed).
- FIRE1 or joystick RIGHT: TRACK highlighted quest, if active.
- FIRE2 or joystick LEFT: CLOSE and resume.
- Touch during inventory/dialogue: ignored by the journal.
- The first touch never selects a quest or closes the journal.

LAYOUT AND WEATHER

- Journal is sized to the 480x320 screen minus the docked HUD area; top docks
  add a top offset, bottom docks reduce height, floating/undocked HUD keeps
  the full 480x320 viewport. When very little room remains, it falls back
  to a full-screen overlay instead of overflowing/clipping its content.
- The modal (Quest Journal OR Adventure dialogue/inventory) temporarily binds
  the world's ORIGINAL palette to remove the day/night/fog tint. The weather
  clock, precipitation, lightning settings and original palettes are unchanged;
  the world renderer resumes the normal tint on the next frame after closing.
- The preview approximates colours for the classic first 16 palette entries.
  Indices above 15 depend on your actual game's palette, and require SIDBOX
  verification. Existing dialogue 9-slice skin is reused, not re-authored.
