GamerGirl Stage 3.63 — Restore passive sliding on narrow slope stamps
===================================================================
Base: Stage 3.62 Player Animation Editor, including Stage 3.61.

PROBLEM
-------
Stage 3.61 stopped a premature tug near cliff edges by requiring an individual
slope/Bezier collision stamp to span >=50% of the player's whole foot width.
For a 20px player on an 8px-wide slippery ramp, this can never happen. The
per-stamp passive force and its zero-damping setting were therefore skipped,
leaving normal friction active. Stage 3.62 did not change the helper.

FIX
---
The passive physics gate now requires at least ONE of the player's central
foot pixels to be horizontally over the specific collision shape. This
preserves symmetric 50%-step-over behaviour at either edge of a larger
surface, but also permits narrow stamps and successive small segments to
trigger their configured slippery/sticky damping. Mirroring is respected.
Normal collision, actual landing, map format, stamp definitions, and exported
animation handling have not changed.

INSTALL
-------
1. Make a backup of your IDE and game project.
2. Replace EXACT IDE source file:
      idelibs/codehelpers/game/tilemap.c
3. In Game Designer, choose Update Game Project to refresh the V1 helper
   installed in your game project (normally gamehelpers/gametilemap.c).
4. Recompile the game and transfer the rebuilt app to SIDBOX.
No SD .map regeneration or GUI rebuild is required for this runtime-only fix
(assuming your game already contains Stage 3.60's surface-enabled map).
