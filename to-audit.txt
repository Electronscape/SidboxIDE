What I'd tackle next
I think we should build on this in three stages:
1. Combat feedback: Optional enemy hit flash, impact effect and knockback. We need to SEE when an enemy takes damage, not just watch it mysteriously expire after four invisible punches. 😂
2. Player attack animation: Allow an attack PPB animation in the Player Profile, with configurable hit timing. No animation should be required if the developer doesn't want one.
3. Visual Nodes integration: Add ON ATTACK, ON HIT and potentially ON MISS events, so attacks can trigger sounds, effects, dialogue, logic and quest events.
