CODERGIRL V1 — SPRITE TWEEN
==========================

Move an existing sprite smoothly between two positions. All times are game
frames: at 50 FPS, 50 frames = 1 second. No heap allocation or firmware changes.

1. Include this at the top of main.c:

#include "gamehelpers/gametween.h"

2. Keep a tween object alive for as long as the sprite moves:

static game_tween_t movement = {0};

3. AFTER gameassets_init(), BEFORE your game loop:

start_tween(&movement, &player, 300, 160, 50, 0);

4. INSIDE the game loop, once per VBlank, BEFORE drawing the sprite:

run_tween(&movement, 1);

Here 'player' is an example name for your own gfxbob_t. The first position
is taken from player.drawx / player.drawy. Loop=0 stops at the destination;
loop=1 continuously repeats the journey.

REPOSITIONING A TWEEN WITHOUT RESTARTING IT
-------------------------------------------
After start_tween(), you can relocate the WHOLE route:

set_tween_pos(&movement, 80, 120);

That changes the route's start to (80,120), shifts its destination by the
same amount, and keeps elapsed time unchanged. For example, an original route
from (10,10) to (110,10) becomes (80,120) to (180,120).

Call set_tween_pos() again whenever you want to move the whole route. It
works during playback and after the tween finishes.

stop_tween(&movement); // Freeze at the current position

Do NOT also run spline/platformer movement on this same sprite at the same
time: both helpers would try to set sprite.drawx / sprite.drawy.

OPTIONAL TWEEN PAUSES
---------------------
A tween can wait BEFORE moving and AFTER arriving. Zero means no wait.
For example, after starting a looping tween:

start_tween(&movement, &platform, 300, 160, 50, 1);
set_tween_pauses(&movement, 25, 50);

At 50 FPS the platform waits half a second at the start, moves for
50 frames, then waits one second at the destination before repeating.
The original start_tween() and run_tween() functions stay unchanged:

run_tween(&movement, 1);

Set the pauses immediately after start_tween(), before your game loop.
Leave this call out to preserve the original, uninterrupted tween.
For a nonlooping tween, the end pause holds the sprite at its destination
before the tween becomes inactive. The movement does not run in reverse.
